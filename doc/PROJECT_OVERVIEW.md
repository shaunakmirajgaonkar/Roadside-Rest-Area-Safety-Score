# Project Overview

The dashboard is designed as screening and decision-support infrastructure for roadside rest-area safety. It intentionally does not replace professional inspections, emergency procedures, road authority standards, accessibility requirements, sanitation regulations, or engineering assessments.

The score combines six transparent pillars:
- Lighting: 18%
- Sanitation: 15%
- Accessibility: 15%
- Crowding: 12%
- Emergency support: 20%
- Verified user-report signal: 20%

Risk bands:
- Low: 80–100
- Moderate: 65–79.9
- High: 50–64.9
- Critical: below 50
